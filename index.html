Questions people ask before ordering

How do Cardly NFC business cards work?

Each card has a small NFC chip inside. Tap it on a modern iPhone or Android phone and your profile, social links, portfolio or menu opens in the browser. No app is needed.

Can customers order directly from the QR menu?

Yes. Customers add items, see an estimated bill while ordering and place the order from their phone. Each order goes to the kitchen as a KOT (kitchen order ticket), which speeds up service and cuts down on mistakes.

How does the encrypted privacy card protect my data?

Your card data is stored encrypted on our secure portal and isn't publicly visible. To see it, a person must enter the access code printed on your card or one you share with them. Without the code, your details stay locked.

What is the smart car card?

It's a card you display on your car. People scan or tap it to reach you about parking, emergencies or other matters to do with your car. With privacy mode, you can hide your details whenever you like and show them again later.

Can I update my details after buying a card?

Yes. Your profile is digital, so you can change your phone number, links, menu items, prices or bio anytime without reprinting the card.
